# Organon: circuit

A directed route with operational flow, gates, or feedback.

## Procedure

1. Identify inputs, components, outputs, and direction.
2. Label gates, delays, feedback, and failure points.
3. Trace one complete pass and a perturbed pass.
4. Record whether the circuit stabilizes, oscillates, or saturates.

## Provenance

Gunkel’s Bright Green discussion names circuitries; procedure is an adaptation.
