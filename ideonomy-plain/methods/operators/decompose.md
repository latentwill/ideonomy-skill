# Decompose

Split a whole into parts, elements, properties, or sub-processes.

## Procedure

1. Choose one relation: part-of, subtype-of, stage-of, or property-of.
2. Recurse until parts are useful.
3. Mark interactions, shared components, and gaps.
4. Reassemble or substitute one part and inspect effects.

## Provenance

Gunkel’s Way 22; tree-finding is the related operator.
