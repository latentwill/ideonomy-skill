# Organon-construction

Choose a form whose structure exposes useful relationships: list for membership, chart for crossed axes, graph for relations, tree for hierarchy, or another catalog form. Define what entries, positions, and links mean; populate the declared scope. Mark sparse regions, unknowns, incompatibilities, and unexamined cases without inferring that a missing instance must exist. Apply further operators to revealing gaps. Construction may precede transformation or organize its results; a second representation is useful only if it reveals something the first obscures.
