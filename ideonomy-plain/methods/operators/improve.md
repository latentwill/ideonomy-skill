# Improve or perfect

Modify a candidate toward a stated goal while preserving constraints.

## Procedure

1. State goal, baseline, constraints, and measure.
2. Locate bottleneck, defect, or limiting tradeoff.
3. Generate local and structural modifications.
4. Compare effects, regressions, and new costs.

## Provenance

Gunkel’s Way 14; perfection is goal-relative.
