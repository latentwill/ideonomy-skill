# Recursive reapplication

Apply an organon to its own outputs or to a new level of the same structure.

## Procedure

1. State the seed organon and recursion rule.
2. Apply once and record the output structure.
3. Apply again only where the output remains interpretable.
4. Stop when the structure stabilizes, cycles, explodes, or loses meaning.

## Provenance

Gunkel’s Yellow Volume glossary names reapplication and recursive use.
