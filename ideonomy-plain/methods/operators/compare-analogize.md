# Compare, analogize, or distinguish

Place cases side by side, transfer only a named relation, and test the limits of the analogy.

## Procedure

1. Choose cases and shared criteria; include a near miss and a distant case.
2. Record similarities, differences, and asymmetries.
3. Transfer one structural relation and state its prediction.
4. Mark where surface resemblance fails.

## Provenance

Gunkel’s Way 2; the cross-domain procedure is an adaptation.
