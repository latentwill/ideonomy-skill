# Find laws or regularities

Search cases for repeatable relations without turning a pattern into a law prematurely.

## Procedure

1. State the candidate relation and searched population.
2. Compare positive, negative, and boundary cases.
3. Check scale, context, and measurement changes.
4. State scope, exceptions, and a disconfirming test.

## Provenance

Gunkel’s Way 7; “law” means provisional regularity here.
