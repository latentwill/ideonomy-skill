# Specialization

Narrow a general idea, dimension, or organon into a more specific branch.

## Procedure

1. State the general form and the property to narrow.
2. Add constraints or domain conditions one at a time.
3. Populate the specialized branch with examples and edge cases.
4. Record what the specialization gains and excludes.

## Provenance

Gunkel’s Yellow Volume glossary and Investigable Dimensions essay name specialization.
