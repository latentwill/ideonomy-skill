# Permutation

Reorder members or dimensions to expose order-sensitive variants.

## Procedure

1. Choose an ordered organon and state what order means.
2. Generate distinct permutations within a bounded set.
3. Compare outcomes and mark which relations change.
4. Assign stable IDs to members and relations; merge only reorderings proven equivalent under that mapping, and retain the equivalence note.

## Provenance

Gunkel’s Yellow Volume glossary names permutation as an organon expansion operation.
