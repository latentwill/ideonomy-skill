# Construct theories, hypotheses, or conjectures

Turn a gap or pattern into a claim with explicit assumptions and a way it could fail.

## Procedure

1. State phenomenon, mechanism, and scope.
2. Separate observations, assumptions, inference, and speculation.
3. Generate competing explanations.
4. Give each candidate a discriminating test.

## Provenance

Gunkel’s Way 4; falsifier discipline is contemporary guidance.
