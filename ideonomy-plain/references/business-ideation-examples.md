# Business ideation examples from session

Use these as compact reference patterns, not fixed recipes. The point is to preserve useful output shapes that worked well in chat.

## Business ideas via periodic grid

User asked for business ideas using ideonomy. Picker selected cross-domain re-instantiation + periodic-grid with naturalness and complexity dimensions.

Useful artifact shape:

```text
Naturalness        | Simple version                 | Complex / AI-enabled version
------------------|--------------------------------|-------------------------------
Fully natural     | Local wild food walks          | Regenerative micro-retreats
Partly man-made   | Backyard garden setup kits     | AI homestead planner
Hybrid            | Human+AI memory coach          | Biofeedback spiritual training
Mostly man-made   | Personal automation concierge  | AI OS for solo founders
Fully synthetic   | Virtual taste/test lab         | Synthetic personality studio
```

Why it worked: the grid forced saturation across two axes instead of a generic list; each cell became a distinct market posture.

## Web design agency leads via tree

User asked for ideas to make leads for a web design agency. Picker selected abstraction-lift + tree with age and distribution dimensions.

Useful lifted form:

> Lead generation is not “finding clients”; it is creating paths where businesses with visible web problems discover, trust, and contact the agency.

Useful tree:

```text
Lead generation for web design agency
|
+-- Concentrated channels
|   +-- High-value lists
|   +-- Partnerships
|   +-- Events
|   +-- Targeted outbound
|
+-- Mixed channels
|   +-- Content + outreach
|   +-- Audits + retargeting
|   +-- Local SEO + direct contact
|   +-- Niche landing pages
|
+-- Distributed channels
    +-- Public tools
    +-- Community presence
    +-- Referral loops
    +-- Automated scans
```

Lead ideas that landed well:

- Ugly Website Bounty List: weekly list of outdated local business sites, sorted by niche; send short Loom teardowns.
- Free Homepage Roast: playful public/private audits with CTA to fix.
- Niche Landing Page Campaigns: SEO pages like “web design for med spas in Austin.”
- Local Business Website Scorecard: grader for speed, CTA, trust signals, booking flow, visual quality.
- Google Maps Opportunity Mining: businesses with good reviews but weak/no website or broken booking.
- Competitor Comparison Audit: show where competitors look more credible or convert better.
- Before/After Redesign Mockups: send a single improved hero screenshot.
- Adjacent Partner Referrals: photographers, brand designers, marketers.
- Website Rescue Offer: urgent fixes for broken booking, mobile, lead capture, abandoned developer.
- AI Website Concierge: website + chatbot + lead capture + booking + review collection.

Why it worked: the tree separated concentrated, mixed, and distributed lead paths, then generated concrete tactics at each branch.
