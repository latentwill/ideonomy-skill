# Naming-style remix note

Session lesson:
- The user may use words like Arc, Rhythm, and Vector as concept buckets in naming work, not as literal title words.
- When they ask for a redo, convert the concept bucket into a fresh title family instead of reusing the bucket label verbatim.
- Prefer actual title families that match the research patterns: Blueprint, Lab, Academy, Method, Protocol, Workshop, Bootcamp.
- Keep the output brandable and human, with the subtitle carrying the explanation.

Useful reminder:
- Distinguish between a *design direction* and a *candidate title*.
- If the user says "drop the guide words," strip the scaffolding and surface the concrete names only.
