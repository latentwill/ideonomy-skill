# Sources and attribution

Sources checked 2026-09-08 except Properties and Dimensions (upstream reference; page unavailable during review). This guide distinguishes source claims from this repository's engineering choices. It is not a transcription or exhaustive survey of the monographs.

| Source | What it supports | Consequence for this adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| Patrick Gunkel, [Introduction to Ideonomy](https://ideonomy.mit.edu/intro.html), “What Is Ideonomy?” and “Nature of Science” | Exhaustiveness is an ideal; the proposed laws are tentative. Inquiry includes criticism and validation. | Bound the explored space, label conjectures, and propose checks. |
| Gunkel, [Subdivisions of Ideonomy](https://ideonomy.mit.edu/division.html), foreword | The 235 listed divisions are provisional; their number depends on purpose. | Eight operators are not a closed canon. Revise classifications when useful. |
| Gunkel, [Dreams for the website](https://ideonomy.mit.edu/dreams.html), newcomer routes | Multiple entry points, navigable scales, and participant-created organons. | Honor user-chosen routes and develop existing artifacts. |
| Grace Kind, [Ideonomy: A Science of Ideas](https://gracekind.net/writing/ideonomy/intro/), “How is ideonomy performed?” | An accessible account of constructing and combining organons. | Retain structured artifacts and credit her synthesis. |
| Kind, [Properties and Dimensions](https://gracekind.net/writing/ideonomy/propertiesanddimensions/) | Properties and substitutions along dimensions. | Use named axes and inspectable transformations. |
| Kind, [Negation in Ideonomy](https://gracekind.net/writing/ideonomy/negation/), “Notating Negation” and democracy example | Property-dependent opposites; illustrative notation and an explicitly invented term. | Distinguish heuristic negation from logical complement and coinage from established usage. |

The [MIT archive](https://ideonomy.mit.edu/legacy-index.html) links the original volumes and charts for deeper work. Its [current introduction](https://ideonomy.mit.edu/) dates Gunkel to 1947–2017 and credits Whitman Richards with hosting and organizing the project. “1937” in the earlier skill was incorrect.

The random tuple mechanism, cooldown, specific 8/17/29 catalog, rendering policies, worked example, and evaluation rubric are contemporary contributions. Method files inherit this distinction unless they identify a particular source. Do not attribute every example or extension to Gunkel or Kind, or treat the archive's historical predictions as established results. Existing repository prose credits and licenses remain in force; links to external works do not relicense those works.
