# Clarify, categorize, survey, and systematize

Turn a fuzzy subject into a bounded, inspectable vocabulary and classification.

## Procedure

1. State the working definition and scope; record ambiguities rather than hiding them.
2. Enumerate kinds, parts, examples, causes, effects, and boundary cases.
3. Choose one relation for the classification and test whether the branches cover the stated scope.
4. Mark provisional, disputed, and missing entries; revise terms only with a stated reason.

## Provenance

Gunkel lists this as Way 1 in “24 Ways to Treat Paths” (Blue Volume).
