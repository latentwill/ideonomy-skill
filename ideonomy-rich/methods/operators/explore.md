# Explore, examine, or perceive

Widen attention before committing to a classification or explanation.

## Procedure

1. Inspect multiple scales, viewpoints, and time slices.
2. Use an unfamiliar domain or conceptual lens.
3. Record anomalies, surprises, absences, and motifs.
4. Convert observations into dimensions, examples, or tests.

## Provenance

Gunkel’s Way 11; unfamiliar-lens recommendation is an adaptation.
