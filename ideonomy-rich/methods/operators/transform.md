# Transform or vary

Remake an idea while tracking what remains invariant.

## Procedure

1. State source representation and properties that must survive.
2. Apply one transformation: transpose, invert, scale, reorder, merge, split, or substitute.
3. Record preserved and broken relations.
4. Test coherence; distinguish known transformations from coined variants.

## Provenance

Gunkel’s Way 19; transformation families are adaptations.
