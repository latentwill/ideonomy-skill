# Critique

Expose assumptions, defects, contradictions, and failure modes.

## Procedure

1. State the candidate and standard it claims to meet.
2. Inspect definitions, hidden premises, omissions, contradictions, and edge cases.
3. Search for counterexamples and dependency failures.
4. Preserve useful parts and propose a targeted repair or rejection.
