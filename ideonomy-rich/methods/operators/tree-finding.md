# Tree-finding

Choose and label the relation: subtype-of, part-of, instance-of, or another stated hierarchy; do not silently mix them. Walk up to useful parents, down to children, across to siblings, and between levels of generality. Record alternatives and gaps at each explored level; stop when further decomposition stops helping the question. Preserve multiple parents as a DAG or graph instead of forcing a tree. Missing branches may reflect incomplete classification or incompatibility, not undiscovered entities. Use siblings for substitution and parent relations for abstraction.

Adapted from Gunkel and Kind; see [sources](../../references/sources.md). [Examples](../../references/operator-examples.md#tree-finding) are optional.
